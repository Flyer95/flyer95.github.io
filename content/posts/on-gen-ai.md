I'm not going to beat around the bush too much here, generative ai sucks. It is the solution to zero problems and the cause of many. It destroys communities, the enviroment, and the brains of those that use it. It devalues everything it comes into contact with and is actively destroying the American economy.

With that being said, I have used gen ai before. Not just early on, when it was a small niche thing that seemed interesting and silly in how poorly it could interpret eating spagetti, but even as recently as this year.

Every so often, when I go down a rabbit hole of some creative endevour I'm working on, I run into a wall. It's a common occurance in learning something new and I'm no stranger to it. Still, I would be lying if I said that I love all of my progress grinding to a halt as I try and figure out why a specific thing isn't working or what exactly I'm missing on how to get something to work.

Give this website, for instance. It's hosted on GitHub using Hugo, which I've messed with before but for the life of me could never get working. I have made, at a bare minimum, 15 different instances of the exact repo this site is hosted on, gone through 4 different themes to find one that I liked, could do everything I wanted it to do, and also be something I understood and could modify if needed. I've been at it for weeks, which makes the fact that it's now here and a thing all the more satisfying, but I almost didn't. I almost went back to just having it through Bear Blog and in the standard, generic (accessible and functional too, I might add) format that it provides. But I wanted something that was uniquely **mine** and could do a bit more, so I kept keeping on.

Now I want to start this next section with a very clear disclaimer. **This website isn't made with any generative ai. I made it with a terminal, a bunch of headaches, and deleted digital files.** Sweat went into making this and I really hope that there aren't tears or blood involved down the line.

I have been told by several friends of mine that ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, etc. are "really bad at (blank), but actually really good at coding". Many of these people are programmers and coders in their respective industries, so I take that with some credibility. I know that ChatGPT doesn't know what a good chord progression sounds like but if these people think it is helpful in their own field, maybe there is something to it. Maybe Generative AI takes up as much water as a small country in a 30 minute session but at least you get a good coding buddy out of it.

So in my darkest moments, when I've tried again and again to troubleshoot a technological issue, searched Reddit and forum posts, watched YouTube videos and tutorials to try and solve this thing to no avail... by brain drifts to the machine that has scraped every webpage on the internet. Maybe... maybe it knows the answer to this question in its massive, polluting datacenter. Maybe it has already gone through and found something that I can not. Maybe it does have knowledge to give me.

And so I sneak away to ChatGPT, this devil that I intend to make a deal with. I believe I have nowhere else to go, nowhere else to turn to. I'm on the end of my ropes and so, I decide to chip away at some of my ethics as payment for this hidden knowledge that I can not find. With it's enthusiastic and kiss-ass tone, it doesn't sound eager to help me. It sounds like it reviles in my suffering, that it knows I hate coming to them but yet I am here anyways. So I ask a single question, putting as much detail as I can in under the potentially false belief that one big message uses less resources than a few smaller ones.

Never once has a LLM delivered.

Every single time it has responded with the most surface-level answer, one that I've already come across, tried, and found not to work. Even when told that "hey, I tried that and it didn't work" it doesn't get any more helpful. It rewords the same message from before, makes up settings and menus that don't exist, and tries so ***god damn hard*** to be bubbly and friendly that it makes me furious. ChatGPT doesn't know that I hate it, that I hate conversing with it. It acts like maybe I could learn to appriciate it, to realize that it's only trying to help me. The audacity.

So I leave the "interaction" worse than when it started, no closer to solving my problem and in a worse mood that before because this *thing* hasn't been able to do anything for me other than waste my time, disolve my sense of ethics, and make me an associate to all the shitty problems it creates.

I didn't even consider ChatGPT as an option this time. When I hit a wall, I looked for any possible hole in it I could potentially squeeze through. I asked my coding friends what to do and if that didn't work, I'd pivot. There are isses with the theme when I try pushing it to GitHub? I'll swap to a different theme, one with less customivation but more simplicity. That's how I've gotten projects done before, anyways. I don't expect it to fail me now.
